# Shushrusha Home Care

Create a modern, clean, and highly trustworthy Home Healthcare & Nursing Service web application named "শুশ্রূষা" (Shushrusha).



### Visual & Design Theme:

- Color Palette: Primary Dark Teal (#0F766E), Secondary Mint Green (#10B981), Soft Gray (#F8FAFC) background, and clean White cards.

- Typography: Clean sans-serif with full support for Bengali typography (Google Fonts like Noto Sans Bengali or Hind Siliguri).

- Overall Vibe: Warm, medical, professional, and easily accessible for families and elderly users.



### Main Navigation & Navbar:

- Brand Logo: "শুশ্রূষা" (Shushrusha) with a subtle medical/care icon.

- Links: Home, Services, Packages, About Us, Contact.

- Action Buttons: Green "WhatsApp Booking" button and a quick "Call Now" emergency button.



### Core Sections:

1. Hero Section:

   - Catchy Headline: "ঘরে বসেই বিশ্বস্ত ও পেশাদার স্বাস্থ্যসেবা"

   - Subheadline: Fast, reliable, and compassionate home nursing and medical support delivered to your doorstep.

   - Primary CTAs: "خدمة বুক করুন" (Book Service) and "WhatsApp Chat".

   - Visual: Modern healthcare illustration/image showing home care nursing.



2. Services Grid (Cards with Icons):

   - Home Nursing (ইনজেকশন, ড্রিপ, স্যালাইন ও ড্রেসিং সেবা)

   - Elderly Care (বয়স্কদের যত্ন ও সার্বক্ষণিক পরিচর্যা)

   - Post-Surgery Care (অপারেশন পরবর্তী রিকভারি সেবা)

   - Emergency Guidance (দ্রুত ডাক্তারি পরামর্শ ও সাপোর্ট)



3. Interactive Booking Modal/Form:

   - Fields: Patient Name, Phone Number, Location/Address, Select Service (Dropdown), Preferred Date & Time, Special Notes.

   - Action: Submit button that formats the request and redirects to a direct WhatsApp message to the admin.



4. Pricing & Service Packages:

   - Single Visit Plan (এককালীন হোম ভিজিট)

   - Weekly & Monthly Care Subscriptions (দীর্ঘমেয়াদী নার্সিং কেয়ার)



5. Why Choose Us (Trust Badges):

   - Verified & Qualified Nurses

   - 24/7 Fast Doorstep Response

   - Affordable & Transparent Costs



6. Footer:

   - Emergency hotline number, service areas, social links, and copyright text.



### Technical & UX Requirements:

- Fully mobile-responsive layout.

- Sticky navbar and a floating WhatsApp quick-chat widget on the bottom-right corner.



- Smooth scrolling effects between sections.
